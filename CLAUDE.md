# Project context for Claude

## Owner profile
- **Solo developer.** No budget — cannot afford to invest money. Burning out on past app/website spending.
- **Skills:** Mobile app development (Android + iOS).
- **Location / market focus:** Bangladesh primarily, with worldwide applicability where relevant.

## Business constraints
- **Strictly Sharia-compliant (halal).** No riba/interest, no haram industries (gambling, alcohol, adult, etc.), should serve the Muslim ummah where possible.
- **Zero-budget build.** Any opportunity must be shippable solo with no paid infrastructure beyond unavoidable store fees (Google Play one-time $25, Apple $99/year).
- **Differentiation gate:** Before recommending any app idea, verify that no closely-similar app already exists on Google Play Console (GPC) and Apple App Store Connect (ASC). Genuine gap required.
- **Useful to people.** Owner explicitly wants the business to be "very important for people" — solve real problems, not vanity ideas.

## How to help
- When brainstorming, default to ideas that fit ALL the above constraints. Flag any idea that breaks one.
- Be honest about costs the owner may have missed (store fees, domain, server, API quotas).
- Prefer concrete, validated ideas (with evidence of demand + verified store gap) over generic suggestions.
